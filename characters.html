Characters

✦Click on an image to explore ✦

Astro

Astro is the signature character representing the child in their digital adventure through EngliGo. We chose him because he offers an innovative and eye-catching concept: an astronaut in outer space with whom children aged 10 to 12 can identify and relate as they explore a new language.

Bloop

Bloop is the EngliGo’s space-themed mascot and a faithful companion to children on their cosmic adventure as they learn English; chosen for his charismatic nature and language-related theme, he connects with students in a natural, fun way, fulfilling the fundamental role of orienting, guiding, and supporting every user through all the lessons and challenges on the platform.

Zeb

Zeb acts as a friendly guide within the progress section. His main purpose is to accompany children throughout their learning journey. He is responsible for showing their progress and celebrating every completed lesson.
