About EngliGo

Learn English, Explore the Galaxy.

🚀 our project

Learning English in a new way.

Engli Go is an educational web platform designed for children aged 10 to 12. Its goal is to make learning English more engaging, visual, and entertaining through the use of technology.

Through activities, characters, and digital resources, students can learn and practice English while interacting with the platform.

🚀

Our mission

To show students aged 10 to 12 that learning English can be fun, modern, and different from the traditional method.

🌌

Our vision

Create a digital space that motivates children to learn English through technology, interactive activities, and entertaining educational experiences.

📖Our history

The Engli Go Journey

Every project stems from a need or an idea. Engli Go was born with the aim of creating a different, engaging, and entertaining way to learn English.

💡
01

The Beginning

Traditional methods are boring and anything but entertaining—especially for children just beginning to delve deeper into English. That is why we decided to create Engli Go: a digital solution designed specifically for them, making the learning experience more modern.

🚀
02

The idea

Engli Go emerged from this need—an educational web platform that combines English learning with technology, interactive activities, characters, and a galactic theme.

💻
03

The development

The project began to take shape through the creation of its visual identity, characters, activities, interface, and digital educational resources.

Each element was designed to create an interesting space that motivates students to participate, explore, and learn.

🌟
04

Our purpose

We aim to demonstrate that technology can become a tool to support English learning by creating new experiences.
